About

Taha Sabry is a Moroccan comic artist and writer, working in comics and manga since 2018. He draws, inks and colors interior pages, covers and character designs, and has collaborated with writers around the world, as well as with local publishers and platforms. He works in English and Arabic.

His short story “Break the Cycle” won the African Talents Award at the 16th Tetouan Festival (2023), and “Absolute Camouflage” placed in the Top 10 at the Tetouan Comics Festival 2024. His work has appeared in the Alkhariqun anthologies, and his one-shot “The Adventures of Sinbad” was published on the Manga Arabia app.

A full page, from blank paper to print-ready files, takes him about 14 hours on average. To keep scenes consistent, he builds 3D models of backgrounds, vehicles, weapons and props in SketchUp, then reuses them from different angles across a story. He also writes his own stories and develops personal projects like Mushroom and Space Pirates, and is open to collaborating with writers and publishers.
